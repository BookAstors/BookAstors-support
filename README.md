## 🧭 About BookAstors

BookAstors is a mobile application designed to simplify salon discovery and appointment booking.  
Users can find nearby salons, select services, choose preferred date and time slots, and book appointments with trusted professionals.

---

## 📱 App Features & How to Use

### 1. 🏠 Salon Browsing & Discovery
**What you can do:**
- Browse and view all available salons in your area
- View salon details including services, ratings, pricing, and location
- Search for salons by name or service type
- View salon images and service descriptions

**How to use:**
- Open the app and navigate to the Home screen
- Browse through the list of nearby salons
- Tap on any salon to view detailed information
- Use the search bar to find specific salons or services
- View salon ratings and customer reviews

**Note:** Salon browsing is available to all users. Login is only required when you want to make a booking.

---

### 2. 📍 Location-Based Services
**What you can do:**
- Find salons near your current location
- View distance and directions to salons
- Get location-based salon recommendations

**How to use:**
- Grant location permission when prompted
- The app will automatically show nearby salons
- Distance is calculated and displayed for each salon
- Tap on salon location to open in maps

**Data Used:** Precise location data (latitude and longitude) is used to show nearby salons and calculate distances.

---

### 3. 🔐 User Authentication & Account Management
**What you can do:**
- Secure login using phone number and OTP verification
- User profile management
- Account security and data protection

**How to use:**
- Enter your phone number on the login screen
- Verify your number using the OTP sent via SMS
- Enter your first name and last name
- Accept terms and conditions to create your account
- Your account will remain logged in across app sessions

**Data Collected:** Phone number, first name, last name, user ID

---

### 4. 📅 Salon Booking Service
**What you can do:**
- Book appointments at your preferred salon
- Select services and time slots
- Manage your bookings (view, cancel, reschedule)
- Receive booking confirmations and reminders

**How to use:**
- Login to your account (required for booking)
- Select a salon and tap "Book Now"
- Choose your preferred services
- Select date and available time slot
- Confirm your booking
- View booking details in "My Bookings" section

**Note:** Login is required for booking as it is an account-based feature that requires user information for booking management, notifications, and order tracking.

---

### 5. 📋 Booking Management
**What you can do:**
- View all your bookings (upcoming, completed, cancelled)
- Track booking status
- Receive push notifications for booking updates
- View booking history

**How to use:**
- Navigate to "My Bookings" tab
- View bookings by status (Upcoming, Completed, Cancelled)
- Tap on any booking to view details
- Cancel bookings if needed (subject to salon policies)

**Data Used:** User ID, booking information, device ID (for push notifications)

---

### 6. 🔔 Push Notifications
**What you can do:**
- Receive booking confirmations
- Get reminders for upcoming appointments
- Receive updates on booking status changes

**How to use:**
- Grant notification permission when prompted
- Notifications are automatically sent for booking-related updates
- You can manage notification preferences in app settings

**Data Used:** Device ID (FCM tokens) for sending push notifications

---

### 7. ❤️ Favorites & Preferences
**What you can do:**
- Save favorite salons for quick access
- Manage your salon preferences

**How to use:**
- Tap the heart icon on any salon to add to favorites
- View your favorite salons in the Profile section
- Remove favorites by tapping the heart icon again

## 🔐 Privacy Policy & Data Collection

### 📊 Data We Collect

#### 1. Contact Information
- **Phone Number:** Used for authentication and account management
- **Name (First & Last):** Used for user identification and booking management
- **Purpose:** App Functionality, Account Management
- **Linked to Identity:** Yes

#### 2. Location Data
- **Precise Location:** Used to show nearby salons and calculate distances
- **Purpose:** App Functionality
- **Linked to Identity:** Yes
- **Note:** Location is only used when you grant permission

#### 3. Device Information
- **Device ID (FCM Tokens):** Used for push notifications about bookings
- **Purpose:** App Functionality
- **Linked to Identity:** Yes

#### 4. User Account Data
- **User ID:** Used for account management and booking tracking
- **Purpose:** App Functionality, Account Management
- **Linked to Identity:** Yes

#### 5. Diagnostic Data
- **Crash Data:** Used to improve app stability and fix bugs
- **Purpose:** App Functionality
- **Linked to Identity:** Yes

### 🔒 How We Use Your Data

- **Authentication:** To verify your identity and secure your account
- **Booking Management:** To process, manage, and track your salon bookings
- **Notifications:** To send you booking confirmations, reminders, and updates
- **Location Services:** To show nearby salons and calculate distances
- **App Improvement:** To fix bugs and improve app performance

### 🛡️ Data Security

- All data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We follow industry-standard security practices
- Your personal information is only accessible to authorized personnel

### 📤 Data Sharing

- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- Data is only shared with Firebase (Google) for app functionality
- We may share data if required by law or to protect our rights

### 💾 Data Retention

- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can request account deletion at any time
- Booking history may be retained for record-keeping purposes

### ✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access Your Data:** View all personal information we have about you
2. **Update Your Data:** Modify your profile information anytime
3. **Delete Your Account:** Request account deletion and data removal (see below)
4. **Opt-Out:** Disable push notifications or location services
5. **Data Portability:** Request a copy of your data

## 🗑️ Account Deletion – BookAstors

Users can request deletion of their BookAstors account and associated data at any time.

### How to Delete Your Account

#### Option 1: Delete Directly from the App

1. Open the BookAstors mobile app
2. Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**
3. Confirm deletion
4. Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted

#### Option 2: Contact Support

Send an email to **bookastros@gmail.com** with:
- **Subject:** Delete My Account
- Your registered phone number
- Confirmation that you want to delete your account

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
